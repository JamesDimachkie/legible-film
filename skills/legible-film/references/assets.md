# Assets: finding and choosing them

How to find footage, stills, music, sound effects, voice, type, 3D, finishing textures and interface art that hold up
in a professional film, and how to clear them. Making assets with generative tools is in [generation](generation.md).
Rights rules are in [claims-and-rights](claims-and-rights.md) §6.

Sources, prices and terms below were read on the vendors' own pages on 2026-10-28. They change often: re-read the
current page before recommending or buying, and treat every price as an example of a level, not a quote.

## 1. The procedure

1. **Write the use** before searching: the end client, paid or organic placement, platforms and accounts,
   territories, dates, cut-downs, languages, and whether the project files will be handed over.
2. **Write the brief for the asset:** its job in the story, subject, light direction, lens perspective, camera
   movement, colour, performance, tempo and usable length. Write the technical delivery separately.
3. **Search three tiers:** a high-quality free source, a subscription library and a premium or per-asset source. Keep a
   comparison sheet of candidate URLs and IDs, creator or shoot, rights gaps, price and the creative reason.
4. **Read the actual asset page and its licence.** A thumbnail or a "royalty-free" label is not evidence. Record what
   is unknown.
5. **Use watermarked previews only as their licence allows.** Put them in the animatic only where the preview licence
   permits it, and never share them outside the project; otherwise use a placeholder and the asset's URL.
6. **Buy at the gate that needs it** ([studio](studio.md) §6), with the person's approval, then replace every comp
   with its licensed final and re-check the final file.
7. **Re-check rights when the use changes:** organic to paid, local to global, a new cut-down or platform, or a
   client asking for the source files.

## 2. Licence questions for every asset

Answer each for the exact item, and write "n/a" rather than skipping one:

- Does the grant cover commercial promotion and **paid placement** (boosted posts, pre-roll, connected TV, screens
  in public)? "Social" and "monetised" are not the same thing.
- Who holds the licence, and can the finished film and its project be handed to the client?
- Territory, term, project, impressions, budget tiers, exclusivity, and whether protection depends on an active
  subscription when the film is published.
- Attribution: is it required, in what wording, and does the frame leave room for it?
- Releases for identifiable people and property, and whether the item is editorial-only. An editorial item is never
  used in advertising, whatever upgrade is bought.
- The role the edit gives each identifiable person: employee, customer, endorser, patient, wrongdoer or illustration.
  A model release does not cover every implied association. Check the vendor's sensitive-use and endorsement clauses
  against the adjacent copy, sequence and voice-over; keep a released face out of a testimonial role unless that role
  is authorised in writing, and otherwise choose a neutral alternative.
- AI use: are editing with AI tools, upscaling, using the asset as a model input, and training each permitted? Stock
  licences often treat these differently, and some forbid them.
- Content ID or other matching: who clears a claim, for which channel, with what proof?
- Does the preview include ingredients sold separately (fonts, music, device artwork)?

Example: Adobe's editorial designation excludes advertising even with an Extended licence, and Pexels warns that its
licence does not cover depicted brands
([Adobe Stock licence terms](https://stock.adobe.com/license-terms),
[Pexels](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042295214-Can-I-use-the-photos-and-videos-for-a-commercial-project)).

## 3. Footage

**Quality.** Judge full-motion previews, not poster frames: a believable action with an editable start and end, held
focus, natural motion blur and handles around the cut. Compare a candidate set for light direction, time of day, skin
rendering, lens perspective, camera height and screen direction, and build a sequence from one shoot where you can.
Inspect the real file (`qc_file.py` reports codec, bit depth, frame rate and colour tags): an editing master such as
10-bit ProRes 422 HQ grades far better than an 8-bit web file, and a washed-out preview is not proof of log footage.
Record each clip's colour interpretation (log, HDR or display-referred, with primaries, transfer and range) for
finishing ([studio](studio.md) §7.9).

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) | atmospheric inserts and textures where release risk is low | free commercial use; no implied endorsement or trademark use | free |
| [Artlist footage](https://artlist.io/help-center/privacy-terms/artlist-license) | repeated production from one consistent library | Pro covers an individual's client work and paid promotion; companies and agencies are pointed to Business plans, with Max Business sold to agencies of any size and required above 50 people across a group ([Max Business](https://help.artlist.io/hc/en-us/articles/29589453909149-Artlist-Max-Business-plan-explained)). Confirm a small agency's plan with Artlist before buying | $31.99/month billed annually ([plans](https://artlist.io/blog/artlist-pricing-and-plans-explained/)) |
| [Stocksy](https://www.stocksy.com/legal/contentlicense) | a few naturalistic shots with a point of view | royalty-free, worldwide, perpetual; clip audio not licensed; AI/ML processing prohibited under the standard terms | $175 for 1080p, $300 for 4K, $400 for 4K log ([pricing](https://www.stocksy.com/pricing)) |
| [Filmsupply](https://www.filmsupply.com/license-terms) | a hero sequence whose light, casting or camera must carry the film | rights-managed per project, client, use and territory | web and social from $219 a clip; wider media by quote ([pricing](https://www.filmsupply.com/pricing)) |

**Search like a professional:** search by light and lens, not by concept: `macro side-lit brushed aluminium`,
`overcast documentary workshop`, `top-down hard light product`, `locked-off city negative space`. Filter for native
orientation, resolution and frame rate, then open the creator's other clips from the same shoot for wide, medium,
detail, entry and exit. A specific, well-observed action beats a vague "innovation" metaphor.

**Reject when:** sharpening halos, hunting focus or exposure, faces or hands that change, interpolated motion, banded
skies, too few handles, unreleased brands, a fake "raw" label, or a beautiful shot that matches neither neighbour. The
first handshake, skyline, whiteboard, server corridor or glowing brain in the results is a cliché unless it has a
precise narrative job.

## 4. Stills

**Quality.** Inspect at the final crop and at 100%: focus on the subject, natural skin and fabric, believable hands and
reflections, no halos, and enough native pixels for the largest crop a pan or zoom will reach. Match light,
perspective, palette and grain across a series.

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license) | landscapes, objects and editorial-feeling atmosphere | free commercial use; releases not guaranteed ([releases](https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612329-releases-and-trademarks)) | free |
| [Adobe Stock](https://stock.adobe.com/license-terms) | broad searches across photos, illustration and vectors | Standard licence covers social and web; audience limits and editorial restrictions apply | 10 credits for $29.99/month on an annual commitment; cancelling early can cost a fee ([plans](https://stock.adobe.com/plans)) |
| [Stocksy](https://www.stocksy.com/pricing) | a small, coherent set with an individual point of view | Standard includes advertising, worldwide, unlimited term | $35 (2 MP) to $135 (12 MP and up) |
| [10M](https://www.10m.co/about) | public-domain art, maps, engravings, botanical plates and posters from 22 museums and archives, searchable by colour, mood, era, medium or artist | rights are stated per work: about 130,000 works carry recorded public-domain or CC0 evidence, and others are display-only or restricted; download only works marked open, and confirm the rights at the institution's own page | free |

**Search** by light and treatment (`soft north window portrait`, `cross-polarised product`, `backlit translucent
glass`, `copy space left`) and stay inside one photographer's session before broadening. **Reject** extreme skin
smoothing, fake depth blur, upscaled crops posing as large files, and posed pointing at screens.

## 5. Music

**Quality.** Choose the structure before polishing sound design: audition the opening, the densest spoken passage, the
key reveal and the ending against the same picture, at matched loudness. Look for a recognisable opening within the
time available, room around speech, usable phrase boundaries and an ending that resolves rather than fading
arbitrarily. Prefer a lossless master and stems, and measure the final mix, not each source track.

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/) | a low-budget piece that can carry a credit | free with Creative Commons credit; a paid licence removes the credit | free with credit |
| [Epidemic Sound](https://www.epidemicsound.com/pricing/) | online client work with a channel-clearing workflow | Pro covers digital ads and freelance client work; published content stays covered after cancellation | Pro $16.99/month, billed $203.88 a year |
| [Artlist music](https://artlist.io/help-center/privacy-terms/artlist-license) | frequent edits where stems matter | the Social plan excludes client projects and paid media; Pro and Business are broader | Music Pro $16.58/month billed annually |
| [PremiumBeat](https://www.premiumbeat.com/license) | a one-off film with versions and stems, no subscription | per track and project; tiers widen the media covered | $59 Standard, $199 Premium, $999 Business |
| [Universal Production Music](https://www.universalproductionmusic.com/en-gb/licensing) | advertising or broadcast needing a specific agreement or commissioned music | licensed by region and use | by quote |

**What the licence must cover:** the composition and the recording, synchronisation to this film, edits and stems,
cut-downs, paid placement, the client's publishing accounts, territory and term. Synchronisation and public
performance are separate questions; ask who handles cue sheets. "Free Creative Commons music" is not enough: CC treats
synchronising music to picture as an adaptation, so ND licences forbid it and NC licences forbid commercial use
([Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#when-is-my-use-considered-an-adaptation)).

**Content ID** can block, monetise or track an upload without knowing the music was licensed elsewhere
([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797370?hl=en)). Clear the client's channel through the library's
own process before release, and record the clearance.

**Search** by function and instrumentation: `minimal pulse no vocals restrained build`, `sparse felt piano`, `dry
analogue synth 100 bpm`, `uplifting without claps`, `15 second sting resolved ending`. Ask for 6, 15, 30 and 60-second
versions, no-melody and no-drums versions, and an isolated ending. **Reject** a consumer streaming subscription
presented as sync permission, a "copyright-free" upload with no chain of ownership, stems promised but absent, and a
brief to imitate a recognisable track.

## 6. Sound effects

**Quality.** Choose by material, action, distance and perspective. Listen for a clean attack, a complete decay, low
noise, unclipped transients and useful variations; prefer lossless files with headroom. Audition alone, under music
and speech, in mono and on a phone.

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Freesound](https://freesound.org/help/faq/) | specific real-world details, room tone, unusual foley | licence varies per sound (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC); uploads can contain unauthorised material | free |
| [Sonniss GDC bundles](https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/) | a broad starting palette | commercial project use without credit; no standalone redistribution (the project team may share them); no AI training | free |
| [Epidemic Sound](https://www.epidemicsound.com/pricing/) | teams clearing music and effects together | included with music on the same plans | as music |
| [BOOM Library](https://www.boomlibrary.com/terms-conditions-regular/) | designed transitions and material recordings with good metadata | worldwide media licence including advertising; no AI/ML training; subscription terms differ from buyouts | prices vary by library and edition |

**Build an event from layers only where each has a job:** the contact or transient, the material body, the air or
motion, and the room or tail. Check the stack for phase cancellation, and never pile full-spectrum impacts on a small
interface change. **Reject** clipped attacks, denoising warble, baked-in music, rips from films or games, voices
hidden in an ambience, and anything NC for advertising.

## 7. Voice-over

**Cast the performance, not the microphone.** Compare short, project-specific auditions with the hardest product
name, a transition and an emotional turn. Ask for a dry sample, and listen on headphones for room reflections,
plosives, clicks, sibilance and over-processing. Agree pronunciation, direction, pickups and timing before recording,
and specify the delivery (for example mono 48 kHz 24-bit WAV, dry, with room tone and alternates). Edit the
recording before any mastering ([studio](studio.md) §7.7).

| Route | Use it for | Usage notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/) (vetted Pro listings) | narrow casting with a dry audition that fits | terms are seller-specific; some listings exclude paid media | listings vary; confirm the package covers the planned use |
| [Voice123](https://support.voice123.com/hc/en-us/articles/205237644-What-is-the-rate-for-a-voice-actor-on-Voice123) | direct auditions and negotiation | fees negotiated; agree media, territory, term and any AI use in writing | by quote |
| [Voices](https://www.voices.com/help/knowledge/faq/Understanding-Licensing) | usage-led casting and budgeting | paid online ads are licensed like broadcast, by market and term | its online-ad jobs average $350-$1,125 ([rates](https://www.voices.com/jobs/online-ad)) |
| Union casting ([SAG-AFTRA](https://www.sagaftra.org/production-center/contract/802/getting-started)) | campaigns requiring union talent | a contract route with session, usage and digital-replica terms | session fees plus usage; a payroll specialist calculates the total |

**Define "buyout"** in the agreement: media, territory, term, cut-downs, translations, and an explicit boundary on
voice cloning, synthetic derivatives and training. **Reject** a spectacular demo with no dry sample, "full rights"
with no term or media, a seller offering someone else's voice, and paid promotion on an unpaid-media licence.

## 8. Type

**Quality.** Test the actual words, numerals, punctuation and languages in the renderer you deliver with: useful
weights, true italics, distinct `I/l/1` and `O/0`, tabular figures for counters, and strokes that survive
compression at phone size ([studio](studio.md) §7.3). Rendering titles into a video, embedding a font in an app and
serving it on the web are separate licences, and passing the font files on with the project is another: where that is
not granted, the handover lists the font, its version and how to licence it ([studio](studio.md) §9).

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) | clear interface labels, product numbers, contemporary titles | SIL Open Font License | free |
| [IBM Plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex) | a coordinated sans, serif and mono family for technical work | SIL Open Font License | free |
| [Source Serif 4](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif/blob/main/LICENSE.md) | editorial headings, a serif counterpoint to interface type | SIL Open Font License, with optical sizes | free |
| [Adobe Fonts](https://helpx.adobe.com/fonts/web/font-licensing/font-licensing.html) | art-direction exploration under an existing Creative Cloud plan | film, broadcast and online video permitted; no app embedding or sharing font files | with Creative Cloud |
| Foundry licences ([Monotype guide](https://www.monotypefonts.com/pages/content/resources-font-licensing-guide)) | brand type and negotiated scopes | desktop, web, app and embedding licensed separately | by quote |

The SIL Open Font License permits titling in commercial video without an artwork credit
([OFL FAQ](https://openfontlicense.org/ofl-faq/)). For a typeface from a directory, read the upstream designer's
licence. Check whether a cloud render server counts as an installation. **Reject** "free for personal use" fonts,
trials, faux bold and italic, and silent fallback glyphs.

## 9. 3D, HDRIs and materials

**Quality.** Inspect the asset in a neutral scene and under the hero lighting: scale and units, pivots, topology,
normals, UVs, texture paths and real subdivision. Light a reflective hero through its reflections
([studio](studio.md) §7.2). For materials, check seams, repetition, texel density and matching
maps; for HDRIs, a true high-dynamic-range original whose light direction matches the scene.

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) | lighting environments, materials, supporting models | CC0, commercial use without attribution | free |
| [Fab](https://www.fab.com/eula) | specific props, environments, specialist models | Standard licence allows commercial use in any tool; no standalone redistribution; CC BY items carry CC terms | per item |
| [Poliigon](https://help.poliigon.com/en/articles/8749749-asset-use-licensing) | consistent materials for repeated renders | advertising included; no sharing of extractable scene files; no AI/ML use; Individual plans are for one person | $27/month Individual, $59/month Business ([pricing](https://www.poliigon.com/pricing)) |

Search for `photogrammetry scanned neutral albedo`, `studio softbox HDRI product` or `real scale subdivision ready`,
and test the intended framing before paying for texture resolution that will never show. For a hero product, use the
manufacturer's CAD or measured modelling, since shape and reflectance carry the claim. A model of a branded device is
separate from the device owner's rules (§11).

## 10. Finishing textures and LUTs

Treat finishing as part of the image pipeline: compare clean and treated versions at full size, at phone size and
after the platform's re-encode ([studio](studio.md) §7.9). Separate a technical transform (log to display) from a
creative look, record each LUT's expected input and output space, and inspect skin, brand colours and gradients.

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procedural (FFmpeg [`noise`](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#noise), [`lut3d`](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#lut3d), an original shader) | subtle, controllable texture tied to the animation | your own code; record its provenance | free |
| [Grainzilla](https://grainzilla.com/) | a small set of real grain scans | commercial use including client work and ads; no redistribution | 1080p pack $24.90; 4K pack $39.90 |
| [CineGrain](https://www.cinegrain.com/texture/35mm) | a deliberately film-derived treatment central to the look | Indie tier capped by production budget; Professional for larger productions, under its own licence | Indie 35mm $96; Professional $999 |
| [FilmConvert Nitrate](https://www.filmconvert.com/nitrate) | exposure-dependent grain and camera-profile film looks | software licence with three activations | from $119 |

**Procedural wins** for a little animated texture, a controlled vignette, an exact brand-colour flare or anything
tied to the motion. A scan wins when its photographed irregularity is visible and meaningful. Compare both at matched
strength and keep whichever serves the picture. **Reject** a LUT with no stated input space, an overlay sold only
through a heavily graded montage, an obvious loop, and any treatment that lowers text or interface legibility.

## 11. Icons, illustration, interface kits and devices

**Quality.** Choose one consistent language: stroke weight, corner radius, perspective, detail and colour behaviour.
Check SVG paths, viewboxes and scaling at the target size, and whether strokes survive your renderer. Interface kits
never stand in for the real product's behaviour ([product-demos](product-demos.md)).

| Source | Use it for | Licence notes | Price level, read 2026-10-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/license) | a consistent, code-native icon vocabulary | ISC licence; keep the notice | free |
| [Envato Elements](https://help.elements.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000628966-Envato-Elements-License) | breadth in illustration, mockups and templates | per-project registration; embedded components may have their own licences | from $16.50/month billed annually ([pricing](https://elements.envato.com/pricing)) |
| [UI8](https://ui8.net/terms) | a coherent illustration set or design kit | marketing videos and client work permitted; no standalone redistribution of the assets | per item |
| [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/) | accurate presentation of a qualifying Apple app | separate conditions per resource | free downloads; terms per resource |

**A house style from public-domain art.** An illustration language can start from a public-domain tradition (woodcut,
engraving, botanical plate, early poster) found in open museum collections. Redraw it as layered vectors in the
brand's colours, so every element can move on its own, rather than tracing one work. Credit the source collection
where it asks, and keep each work's rights record in the asset register.

**Device and platform owners have their own rules.** Apple's App Store marketing guidance forbids modifying, spinning
or animating its supplied product images and 3D simulations of its products in that context, and requires trademark
credit lines in video ([guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/)). Google forbids
implying an endorsement that does not exist ([brand guidance](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/)).
A marketplace seller cannot grant a manufacturer's rights. When permission for a recognisable device cannot be
established, use an honestly generic device or a plain screen presentation.

## 12. Budget ladder

Planning envelopes for one film of roughly 30-90 seconds, excluding labour, media spend and tax. They are starting
points to agree with the person, not market quotes; one correctly scoped subscription can cover several rows.

| Category | Modest | Serious | No-compromise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Footage | $0-150: free stock, fewer shots, careful matching | $300-1,500: a few bought shots or a scoped plan, money kept for the hero sequence | $2,000+: a coherent hero shoot or rights-managed sequence |
| Stills | $0-100 | $100-500: one cohesive bought session | $1,000+: commissioned casting, product and retouching |
| Music | $0-100: credited free or a one-project licence | $200-1,000: a scoped plan or extended single-track rights, with stems | $2,000+: negotiated library rights or original composition |
| Sound effects | $0-100 | $150-500: one or two coherent libraries | $1,000+: custom foley and a dedicated sound-design pass |
| Voice | $0-250: an agreed in-house read or narrow booking | $350-1,500: cast, directed, specified usage | $2,000+: premium or union casting with campaign terms |
| Type | $0: a tested open family | $50-300: a family with video rights | by quote: brand, app and server scope |
| 3D and materials | $0-100: CC0 environments and materials | $100-500: a hero model or material set | $1,000+: accurate modelling and look development |
| Finishing | $0-50: procedural or a modest pack | $100-300: selected scans or a film-emulation tool | $1,000+: wider scan licensing and a colourist |
| Icons and interface | $0-100 | $100-500: a matched, fully cleared kit | $1,000+: original illustration and brand permissions |

Spend on the film's actual bottleneck first ([studio](studio.md) §6). Count a subscription at its full commitment
(annual terms, minimum periods, tax), not one month's charge. Unresolved permission is a reason to hold or replace an
asset, never to spend the remaining budget on polish.

## 13. The asset record

Keep one row per asset in [assets.csv](../assets/templates/assets.csv), with the evidence beside the project. The
template's columns are the minimum: add columns, or link a licence record per asset, until everything below is held.
A filled row missing any of it is not evidence of clearance.

- **Identity:** title, ID, creator, the item's URL, vendor, file name, version, hash and date acquired.
- **Grant:** the licence and its version as read, licensee and end client, project, seats, paid or organic media,
  territory, term, limits, handover and AI restrictions.
- **Role:** for each identifiable person, the role the edit implies and the clause or written permission that covers
  it.
- **Proof:** receipt or the no-fee grant, certificate, project registration, release evidence, written permissions,
  expiry and renewal dates.
- **Credit:** the exact required line and where it appears.
- **Release check:** final file name and timecodes, licensed final confirmed in place of the comp, transformations
  allowed, Content ID clearance where relevant, and any unresolved restriction marked **hold, do not publish**.
