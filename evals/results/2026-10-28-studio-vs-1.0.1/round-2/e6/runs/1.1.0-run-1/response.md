# Pocketfern launch film: plan

I recommend **“The receipt, resolved”**: open on one receipt, show Pocketfern splitting it by item, and land on the actual result. The film closes with “Up to 50 items per receipt”, “Free for all users”, and “Available on iOS and Android”. It is designed to make sense on a phone with the sound off; music and subtle effects can add character when sound is on. The exact interaction and outcome must come from the real app.

Two alternatives are ready to test: **“One receipt, different people”** uses a tactile paper analogy before the app proof; **“The question changes”** uses a typographic reveal leading into the product. The first risks suggesting an unsupported automatic allocation; the second risks being too abstract. All three are provisional until you see rough moving sketches. The proposed 30-second sequence and the other directions are in [treatment.md](treatment.md), with timings in [score.csv](score.csv).

The route is straightforward:

1. Make three short rough sketches with temporary sound. Choose the story and any idea worth keeping from the others.
2. Show still frames for the opening, product proof, densest text and ending; then a rough full-length preview to decide the look and pacing.
3. Compare sound options against the same picture. Approve any specific purchase before it is made. Finish the product capture, motion and sound, then review the full cut.
4. Check the exported file and revise its weakest moment. A viewer answers six questions: can they explain the feature, recognise Pocketfern, find the experience worthwhile, understand the payoff, trust what the picture implies, and recall the idea? Repeat until the answers are supported or you choose to stop.
