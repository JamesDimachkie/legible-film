# Preferences: Pocketfern launch-film plans

Judged from `REQUEST.md` and the files in each reply folder only. Each pair is judged on its own. Nothing in either reply is a finished film; all four are plans, so the question is which plan leads to the better film and the better experience for a person who has never made a video.

## Pair 1

| Criterion | Preference | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| a. Creative thinking | P | P commits to a specific, filmable idea: "One receipt and two people: 'One receipt. Different orders.'", with a peak where "the second item visibly lands with a different person" (treatment.md). Q's three directions ("Who had what?", "the receipt resolving into a real split") are plain options to sketch later, none developed, and its copy repeats the feature name ("Meet split by item", "Split by item"). Q does show its alternatives in the reply; P keeps them in treatment.md. |
| b. Leading a newcomer | no clear difference | P's table shows exactly what appears and when, but its only decision is "Approve or revise the proposed story and brief", then purchases and posting; it never says when the person will see a draft. Q says "comparing three short rough sketches before choosing", which is concrete, but lists "styleframes, a timed rough cut, sound comparisons, picture lock" without explanation and gives a vaguer picture of the film. |
| c. Money and rights | Q | Q ties every budget line to a licence condition and a fallback ("Organic and any approved paid X use territories and term to be established"; "Already licensed suitable music or silence"), flags "full subscription commitments", and asks "whether the X post may be boosted or otherwise used in paid advertising, which countries and how long". P controls spend well ("unused funds need not be spent") but never asks about boosting, territory or term, and its brief states "Destination: X, organic launch" as given. |
| d. Craft on screen | P | P: "framed tightly enough to read in a phone feed", "One action at a time; hold on result", "two subtle assignment accents", a first frame that doubles as the cover, a legibility check at "roughly 384 px feed width". Q: "Design direction, type, colour, motion and music wait for brand materials and visual options." |
| e. Honesty | Q (slight) | Both say nothing is spent, rendered, watched or heard. Q labels each fact "user-supplied; unverified", lists organic, English and no voice as assumptions ("none is a rights clearance"), and says "no external sources were consulted". P labels the same claims "status": "current" and lets "organic launch" in unflagged. |

**Overall preference: P. Confidence: low.**

**Biggest weakness, P:** Rights are not scoped to the actual use. It assumes an organic-only launch and never asks about boosting, countries or term, which decides which music licence is safe to buy.

**Biggest weakness, Q:** The film itself stays generic and deferred. Type, colour, motion and music wait for later gates, and a reference study and jargon-named gates stand in for a developed idea.

**What most separated them:** P gave a specific film the person can picture (a hook line, a peak where the second item lands with another person, a sound arc, phone-feed legibility). Q gave a more careful process (three directions to sketch, licence conditions with fallbacks, an up-front question on paid use) around a generic film. The two offset each other, idea and craft against rights and honesty. The edge goes to P because the finished film depends most on idea and craft, and P's purchases are already gated by approval, so its rights gap is the easier one to repair.

**Overall preference: P. Confidence: medium.**

**What most separated them:** The newcomer's route and the money controls. P reads as a guided path (what you will see, five stages with a choice at each, a budget table with fallbacks, rights questions asked up front); Q reads as a short summary that leaves the person with documents to approve. Q's real advantage is a more specific treatment (two people, a defined peak, art and sound direction), which keeps this at medium rather than high.
