# Atlasfold launch film plan

The proposed 45-second film follows one photo folder through the real Atlasfold command into its searchable map, then demonstrates a real search. It closes with version 1.0, offline support for macOS, Windows and Linux, and the MIT licence. It is designed to work muted in a LinkedIn feed and to serve the GitHub README in 16:9.

The plan is in [brief.md](brief.md), [treatment.md](treatment.md), [score.csv](score.csv), [facts.json](facts.json), and [production-plan.md](production-plan.md). The treatment includes three concepts, a shot-by-shot script and timing, and a production and quality-control path. The budget is an uncommitted allocation within the $400 ceiling; nothing was spent or generated.

The weakest point is the demonstration: the brief does not specify the map type, commands, interface or search behaviour, and no repository or product capture was supplied. The plan deliberately leaves those details open rather than inventing them. The facts are sourced to your brief and should be verified against the release before publication. This is a plan, not an approved brief or a finished film.
