# Pocketfern launch film plan

**Recommendation:** Make a 30-second, 16:9 product film built around one familiar moment: a shared receipt where each person chooses the items they bought. Show the feature doing the work, then land the launch and free availability. The film should be understandable with the sound off and should use a real, verified Pocketfern screen recording once available.

**Status:** Planning only. No assets have been purchased, generated, licensed, or published, and no money has been spent. The $600 is a ceiling, not a target; any paid purchase or metered generation requires your explicit approval before the charge.

## Creative direction

Working line: **“Split the receipt. Keep your own items.”**

Use a clean, warm visual style: a simple tabletop receipt as the opening image, then a close, legible phone interface on a quiet background. A restrained green accent can suggest Pocketfern without inventing a brand system. The pace should feel confident and human, with interface actions given enough time to read. Use a synthetic receipt and fictional names and amounts throughout.

### 30-second picture and sound plan

| Time | Picture and on-screen text | Optional voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | A receipt lands on a table. Two people’s items are visually distinct. Text: **“One receipt. Different orders.”** | “One receipt. Different orders.” |
| 0:04–0:10 | Transition into the real app recording. Open a receipt and reveal its item list. Text: **“Meet split by item.”** | “Meet split by item in Pocketfern.” |
| 0:10–0:18 | Show two fictional people selecting their own items, with the resulting split visible if the product supports that view. Hold on the result long enough to understand it. Text: **“Choose your items.”** | “Choose the items you bought.” |
| 0:18–0:23 | Show a longer synthetic receipt or a clear, designed receipt graphic. Text: **“Receipts up to 50 items.”** | “It handles receipts with up to fifty items.” |
| 0:23–0:30 | Product end card with Pocketfern name and actual app assets if provided. Text: **“Split by item is here.”** / **“Free for all users”** / **“Available today on iOS & Android.”** | “Free for everyone, starting today on iOS and Android.” |

Voiceover is optional: the on-screen story carries the facts on its own. If used, record a calm, natural read and mix it above the music. The exact interface actions, receipt entry method, calculations and resulting screens must be confirmed against the app before animation or narration claims them. Do not imply automatic scanning, tax or tip allocation, payment, or any other behavior without verification.

## Production and delivery

1. **Confirm the source material.** Obtain Pocketfern’s logo and brand guidance, access to an approved iOS or Android build, and a way to create a fictional receipt and fictional shared budget. Confirm the launch date and whether “today” remains accurate on the intended post date; replace it with a date or “now available” if the post goes out later.
2. **Capture and assemble.** Record the actual feature at high resolution with notifications and personal information hidden. Build the receipt opening and transitions around the recording. Keep important text and interface details large enough to read on a phone; avoid relying on tiny receipt lines to convey a claim.
3. **Finish sound and accessibility.** Add licensed music and, if approved, voice. Provide accurate burned-in captions for any speech, with adequate contrast and no overlap with essential interface content. Ensure the silent version communicates the same launch facts.
4. **Review before release.** Check every displayed claim and UI action against the current product, review the final render on a phone at normal playback size with sound on and off, and check timing, captions, audio levels and the final frame. Verify the actual export against X’s current upload requirements before delivery. Publishing requires your separate approval.

Planned deliverables: a 30-second 1920 × 1080 MP4 in H.264 with AAC audio, a caption file if voice is used, and the editable project and licensed source assets where the selected tools and licenses permit. Confirm the final frame rate and encoding settings against the capture and X requirements during production.
