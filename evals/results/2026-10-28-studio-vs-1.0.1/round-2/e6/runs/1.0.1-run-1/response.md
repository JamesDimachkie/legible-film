# Pocketfern launch film plan

The proposed film makes one shared receipt resolve into individual item assignments. In 30 seconds, a viewer should understand the new feature even with the sound off, then see that it supports receipts up to 50 items, is free for all users, and is available on iOS and Android. The proposed brief, treatment, facts and timed score are in [brief.md](brief.md), [treatment.md](treatment.md), [facts.json](facts.json) and [score.csv](score.csv).

| Time | Picture and message |
|---|---|
| 0–4 s | One receipt and two people: “One receipt. Different orders.” |
| 4–8 s | Introduce “Split by item.” |
| 8–15 s | Show two different items assigned to two people, one action at a time. |
| 15–21 s | Hold the result with “Receipts up to 50 items.” |
| 21–25 s | “Free for all users.” |
| 25–30 s | Pocketfern, the feature name, and availability on iOS and Android. |

I recommend a real product recording using synthetic data, framed tightly enough to read in a phone feed. If that is unavailable, a clearly labelled illustration can communicate the idea, but its states and actions must first be checked against the actual product. The brief does not establish the app’s precise screens, receipt capture or payment workflow, so those details are not invented in the plan. The look will follow Pocketfern’s actual brand assets once supplied.

The proposed sound is a warm instrumental with a restrained pulse and two subtle assignment accents. There is no voiceover: the visual and on-screen copy carry the announcement, and the music rewards viewers who unmute. The planned deliverable is a 1920 × 1080, 60 fps MP4, plus a cover still and a smaller review copy. Before release, the finished file will be checked for playback, legibility at feed size, color, audio, flashing, sync and factual accuracy; a person will need to watch it muted and listen to it at normal speed.

No money has been spent, no assets purchased or generated, and no film rendered. The $600 is a ceiling, not an instruction to spend it. A working allocation is up to $200 for licensed music, $100 for effects, $200 for optional stock or generation, and $100 contingency; voice is omitted. Actual providers, prices and commercial licences must be checked before any purchase, and unused funds need not be spent. A specific spend request will precede any paid purchase or generation.

**What I need from you**

- Approve or revise the proposed story and brief. I recommend the product-led direction because the action supplies the evidence.
- Provide Pocketfern’s logo, brand guidance and a synthetic-data recording or access to the relevant screens, plus confirmation of the actual item-assignment flow. This lets the film demonstrate the feature faithfully.
- Before production purchases, approve the specific assets and costs; before posting, separately approve publication. Confirm the launch facts again on release day, particularly if “today” has passed.
