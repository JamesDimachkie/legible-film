# Pocketfern launch film brief — proposed, awaiting agreement

Deliverable: one 30.0 s, 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 60 fps H.264 MP4, BT.709 SDR, AAC stereo 48 kHz; a matched lightweight review copy and cover still. Music and restrained effects; no voice proposed, so no speech caption file is needed. Make the whole story legible muted. No loop or additional aspect ratio is commissioned.

Audience: people sharing expenses who encounter the announcement in an X phone feed. They should understand that Pocketfern now lets them split a receipt by item, because they see items assigned to different people. The film does not establish automatic scanning, tax or tip allocation, payment, calculation rules, or a particular interaction. Job: announce and explain, then invite the viewer to find the feature.

Fixed: user-supplied facts in facts.json: launch today on iOS and Android; up to 50 items per receipt; free for all users. Source is the user's brief, not independent product verification. Brand assets, app screens, actual workflow, launch date evidence and landing URL have not been supplied. Creative and factual approval: user. Assume organic X placement; confirm licensing also covers any paid promotion if planned. User's $600 is an upper budget, not authorization to spend; spend to date $0.

Evidence: use a real, sanitized screen recording only after validating the workflow. Until then, illustrations are visibly labelled “Illustrative”; examples and names are fictional, and no invented UI is presented as a recording. Use no personal or customer data. No private material leaves the machine without explicit approval.

Checks before delivery: verify source claims and asset rights; check master specification, decode, BT.709 conversion/tags, flash, blanks, motion, onsets and sync, loudness and true peak after re-encode, full-resolution and phone-size frames, all text and claims. A named person watches the actual delivered file muted at phone size, then with sound and pauses for notes. Reviewers and device to be named. Release requires explicit approval. X's current official upload requirements must be checked before final encoding; 60 fps and format are provisional.

Assumptions: audience is existing and prospective users; no supplied brand kit or capture; no voice; film starts as a concise product demonstration. Approvals of brief and story remain open.
