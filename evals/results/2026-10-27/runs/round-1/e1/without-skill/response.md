# Pocketfern launch film plan

Create one 30-second film in two independently composed versions: 16:9 for X and 9:16 for Instagram Stories. The story is a familiar shared meal: one receipt, different orders, a clear way to assign each item. The film ends on the three launch facts and a Pocketfern call to action.

## Creative direction

A warm, tactile tabletop setting gives way to a crisp, legible app demonstration. Use a small illustrative receipt with three items and two people so the assignment is understandable at a glance. The core idea is: “Split what you ordered.” Use restrained motion, close crops, and a single accent color. Music should feel light and optimistic; subtle receipt and tap sounds can support the action. The film must also work silently through on-screen text.

The actual Pocketfern interface, branding, and approved assets have not been supplied. The interface described below is a storyboard placeholder, not a claim about the app's exact workflow. Before production, replace it with a verified recording or approved mockup; do not imply that scanning, automatic recognition, tax allocation, or payment features exist unless confirmed.

## Timing and script

| Time | Picture and motion | On-screen text | Optional voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | A receipt lands between two friends' meals. Push in on the different items. | One receipt. Different orders. | “One receipt. Different orders.” |
| 0:04–0:08 | The receipt transitions into the Pocketfern interface. The item list appears. | Meet split by item | “Meet split by item in Pocketfern.” |
| 0:08–0:16 | Show a real or approved interface assigning individual items to each person, one at a time. Hold long enough to see the result. | Split what you ordered. | “Split what you ordered, item by item.” |
| 0:16–0:21 | The completed assignment stays visible; a short fact card enters. | Receipts up to 50 items | “For receipts with up to 50 items.” |
| 0:21–0:26 | Transition to a simple product end card. | Free for all users | “Free for all users.” |
| 0:26–0:30 | Pocketfern wordmark and call to action. Keep the card still enough to read. | Split by item is here today.\nAvailable on iOS and Android.\nOpen Pocketfern | “Available today on iOS and Android.” |

If the film is published after launch day, replace “today” in the end card and voiceover with “now”; confirm the intended publication date before locking copy.

## Format and delivery

- **X:** 1920 × 1080, 16:9. Compose the tabletop and interface side by side where useful, with large text and generous margins.
- **Instagram Stories:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16. Reframe the same beats vertically; stack the receipt, interface, and text rather than cropping the landscape master. Keep essential text and the call to action away from platform overlays at the top and bottom.
- Deliver each as a 30-second H.264 MP4 with stereo audio, plus a silent-friendly version if needed. Use burned-in text for the essential message; add captions for any voiceover without obscuring the interface. Check current platform specifications before export.

## Production and review

1. Obtain the Pocketfern wordmark, brand guidance, and a current iOS or Android screen recording showing item assignment. Verify that the recording represents the released feature and that any receipt and names are synthetic or approved for use.
2. Build a timed storyboard and animatic for both aspect ratios. Confirm the actual interaction and the readability of each fact before adding polish.
3. Produce the two compositions, using licensed or original music and sound. Align any narration to the timecoded script and leave room for the end card.
4. Review both exports at phone size and with sound off. Check timing, interface accuracy, text legibility, overlay clearance, audio levels, and the three supplied launch facts. Verify the final files' duration, dimensions, and playback before delivery.

No film has been rendered. The main dependency is an accurate view of the released interface and Pocketfern's visual assets; without them, the app demonstration cannot be represented faithfully.
