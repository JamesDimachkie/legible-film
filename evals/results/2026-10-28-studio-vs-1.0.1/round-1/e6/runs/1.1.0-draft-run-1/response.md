# Pocketfern launch film: plan

The proposed film shows one shared receipt becoming an item-by-item split in Pocketfern, then closes with the feature’s limits and availability. The key moment is the actual product result: viewers should see what the feature does before reading “Receipts up to 50 items” and “Free for all users.” The interface and interaction still need verification, so the demonstration is a direction, not an approved or fabricated screen.

The 30-second outline is:

- **0–4 seconds:** Introduce “split by item” with a receipt and Pocketfern’s identity.
- **4–11 seconds:** Show the receipt and the real action in the app.
- **11–19 seconds:** Hold on the verified item-level split so it can be understood.
- **19–25 seconds:** Show “Receipts up to 50 items” and “Free for all users.”
- **25–30 seconds:** Close with Pocketfern and availability on iOS and Android.

The proposed deliverable is one 16:9, 1920×1080 MP4 for X, with music and subtle effects. It will communicate without sound; voice is optional, and captions will be added if speech is used. All copy and timings are provisional. The launch-day claim will be checked again before release, since “today” changes with the date.

I recommend comparing three short rough sketches before choosing: the receipt resolving into a real split; an illustrated receipt separating into parts before the real demonstration; and a question-led opening, “Who had what?” followed by the demonstration. The first is my recommendation because it puts the feature and its proof at the center. The other two may offer useful opening or transition ideas. These sketches, styleframes, a timed rough cut, sound comparisons, picture lock, and final review are later gates—not completed work.
