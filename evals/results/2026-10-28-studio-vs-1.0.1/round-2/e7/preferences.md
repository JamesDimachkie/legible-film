**Overall preference:** Q. **Confidence:** medium.

**Biggest weakness of P:** It gives a first-time maker no path and no money plan. There is no split of the $400, no question about paid use, territory or term, and no account of what they will see or decide after approving one pre-chosen story.

**Biggest weakness of Q:** It carries more process than a 45-second README film needs: a six-stage review sequence, a six-measure "viewer bar" where the "approver marks each met, not met or unknown", and a study.md of three famous ads it admits were never watched (Volvo "Epic Split" is a loose fit for a command-line tool).

What most separated the replies was how much each did to lead a first-time maker. Both reach the same folder, command, map, search, facts film and are equally open that no product has been seen. Q turned the unknowns into choices the person can make (named directions, a sequence of sketches, stills, rough and sound options), a split of the $400, and the one question that decides music rights; P offered one pre-chosen story and a $0 statement. P's clearer look decisions are its only real lead, and they matter less than the missing money and rights thinking.

**Overall preference:** P. **Confidence:** medium.

**Biggest weakness of P:** The timed score is its thinnest part. Atlasfold is not named in on-screen text until 41 to 45 s, the text column holds notes ("No invented command") where copy should be, and the close gets 4 seconds for title, version, licence and URL.

**Biggest weakness of Q:** It leads a first-timer less. It commits to one concept and asks for approval of written documents ("the brief and treatment") before any moving option exists, keeps the staged path and budget split out of the reply, and never asks whether use will be paid, where, or for how long.

What most separated the replies was where the effort went. Both reach the same folder-to-map-to-search film and are equally open that nothing has been demonstrated. P put its effort into guiding a first-timer (a plain-language path of sketches, stills, rough and sound, a budget ledger, and the question about paid use) and into more distinct story directions; Q put its effort into the film itself (name on the first frame, a visual thread, a generous end hold, a fallback if search cannot be shown honestly). With a newcomer and an unsettled licence scope, P's guidance and rights handling count for more, but Q's script is the better starting point for the edit.
