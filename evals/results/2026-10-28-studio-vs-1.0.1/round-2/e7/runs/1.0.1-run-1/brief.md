# Atlasfold launch film — draft brief

Deliverable: 45 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 30 fps, H.264 MP4, BT.709, AAC stereo 48 kHz; one master for LinkedIn and README use, cover still, and SRT if speech is used. Music and restrained effects planned; narration optional. Verify current destination requirements before encoding. README video embedding and hosting depend on the repository and hosting choice.

## Change
Audience: developers and technically curious photo owners seeing a muted feed or a repository. They should understand that Atlasfold is a command-line tool that turns a folder of photos into a searchable map, because they see the input, real invocation and resulting search. Likely misunderstanding: that the map is a geographic map, or that search understands image content. Neither is established. This film does not establish speed, search semantics, supported formats, privacy guarantees beyond offline operation, or installation simplicity. Job: explain and invite a repository visit.

## Fixed and evidence
Facts are in facts.json, sourced to the user's brief, not yet independently verified. No product assets, commands, source repository, brand files or demonstration data were supplied. The demonstration must be a recording of verified 1.0 behavior with synthetic or licensed photos; any reconstruction or illustrative transition is labelled. Do not imply time savings through compressed processing; label any time compression.

## Destination and assumptions
Must work muted at phone feed width; no loop required. The cover and closing card identify Atlasfold. No URL or install command is invented. 30 fps is a provisional compatibility choice; test motion and verify current LinkedIn requirements before export. README playback mechanism and hosting remain to be confirmed.
