# Pocketfern launch film: plan

The proposed film follows one receipt as its individual items are split, then ends with the three facts viewers need: **up to 50 items per receipt, free for all users, on iOS and Android**. The product interaction will come from a real recording or a verified reconstruction; the brief does not establish how that interaction works yet.

This is a plan, not an approved treatment or finished film. Nothing has been purchased, generated, uploaded or published. The $600 cap is a ceiling, not permission to spend it.

## What the viewer will see

| Time | Proposed moment | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | A receipt and “Split by item” | Make the announcement immediately legible in a muted feed. |
| 3–8 sec | The receipt in Pocketfern | Establish what is being split. |
| 8–17 sec | The verified item-splitting interaction and its result | Show how the feature works; hold the result long enough to read. |
| 17–22 sec | The result with “Receipts with up to 50 items” | Show the limit in context. |
| 22–27 sec | “Free for all users · iOS + Android” | State availability. |
| 27–30 sec | Pocketfern and “Split by item” | Leave a clear, memorable close. |

These timings and words are provisional. The film should make sense with sound off. My starting recommendation is music and subtle interaction sounds without narration, subject to a real viewer understanding the demonstration. No gesture, automatic receipt scanning, tax handling or settlement behavior will be invented.

## How we get from here to a finished film

1. **Study and evidence.** Research 5–8 documented comparable films and X's current video requirements; annotate specific moments and select two or three references with you. Obtain the actual product flow and brand material. This study is still open, not completed.
2. **Choose a story by watching.** Make three short rough sketches: a receipt resolving into the real product flow; individual receipt lines as a visual analogy followed by the product; and a shared total reframed as item choices. My provisional preference is the first because it puts the feature itself at the center. Choose after seeing them, with temporary sound clearly marked.
3. **Choose the look and pace.** Show opening, proof, dense-information and ending stills at full size and phone-feed size. Then show a complete rough timed film, or animatic, with temporary sound. Review the order and readability before polishing motion.
4. **Choose sound and finish.** Compare two loudness-matched music directions against the same picture. Once the picture is approved, finish the motion and mix, clear each asset for the actual use, and export a 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 16:9 H.264 MP4 and cover still. Add captions if voice becomes necessary.
5. **Check and review.** Check the exported file's decoding, format, color, contrast, text, flashes, motion, audio sync and peaks, including after a lossy re-encode. Recheck every claim and licence. A named person should watch the final file once muted at feed size, then with sound, and flag anything that feels wrong. A viewer from the intended audience should explain what happened before being told. Posting needs your separate approval.

I can prepare the options and measure the file, but I cannot watch motion at normal speed or hear it. Until a person does both, a cut will be reported as **unwatched** and **unheard**. The sketches, styleframes and animatic are rough previews; each decision affects later work, and changing the picture after it is locked can require sound work again.
