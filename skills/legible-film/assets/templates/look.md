# Look

What the person wants this film to look, sound and move like. References carry the look better than words, so this
file says what to take from each, what to avoid and what the finished film should be able to sit beside. Every stage
reads it; the person's notes in the Screening Room outrank it. Delete every line that does not apply: a stray line
steers every later stage.

## Avoid

- The person's own bans:
- Defaults that make generated work look generic; each stays out unless chosen for this film: a small label over a
  headline over a subhead; monospace capitals as decoration; a serif or display font picked at random; beige or a
  purple gradient as the default colour; 01 / 02 / 03 counters; glowing orbs, shockwave rings and drifting
  particles; em dashes in on-screen copy; one easing curve on every move; interface screens tilted in a stock
  perspective; a stock opening fade and a centred closing card.
