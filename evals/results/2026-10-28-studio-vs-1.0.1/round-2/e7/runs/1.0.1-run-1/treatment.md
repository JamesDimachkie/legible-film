# Treatment — From folder to findable

## Change and boundary
A viewer sees a folder become a searchable map through an actual Atlasfold invocation, then sees a search yield a real result. This proves only the behavior recorded under the stated conditions; map semantics and search features await verification.

## Three concepts
1. **Product in use:** a folder, a command, a map, a search and a result. Its strength is a visible causal chain; the risk is an unreadable terminal.
2. **Relational analogy:** photo tiles gather into an ordered field. A familiar visual shift makes the transformation intuitive, but could imply a geographic map or invented organization. Label the transition illustrative and stop the analogy before the real output.
3. **Reframe:** begin on the result, then trace it back to its photo folder and command. It creates curiosity, but risks obscuring the action that produced it.

Choose product in use, borrowing the tile transition and the result-first glimpse. Governing operation: an unstructured-looking folder becomes an observable, searchable output; the same sample photos anchor the beginning and result. The transition is an illustration, not proof of an internal process. Keep sample identity stable.

## Tests — provisional
- Operation: input → invocation → output → search should read even as four stills; test with a newcomer.
- Truth: real recorded command and output must be confirmed against 1.0; do not draw a specific map or invent a query until known.
- Decoding: a first viewer should be able to describe the change; untested.
- Specificity: the real CLI and real output distinguish Atlasfold from a generic photo library; depends on obtaining the product.
- Muted: screen labels and staged interaction carry cause and result; verify at phone size.

## Structure
A result-first glimpse opens, then the folder sets up the action. The turn is the real command; the peak is the search producing a visible result. The final card identifies version and license and directs the viewer to the repository, once its URL is known. No promise of speed or search semantics. Exact wording and timeline are in score.csv and facts.json.

## Look and sound
A restrained dark terminal canvas and warm photo surfaces are provisional, pending actual brand assets. One accent marks the current action; no invented logo. Use large cropped terminal/output regions, not a tiny whole desktop. The music supports a steady rise and resolves on the result; sparse, soft interaction accents follow real events. No voice is necessary to understand the film. License-check all assets for both destinations before acquisition.

Cover: an Atlasfold title and the line “A searchable map from a folder of photos,” with a small illustrative photo arrangement; verify at feed size.

Open: repository URL, real v1.0 commands and output, search behavior and map type, brand files, usable sample photos and asset rights. Exact demo text is intentionally withheld until verified. The draft brief and story await the owner's agreement.
