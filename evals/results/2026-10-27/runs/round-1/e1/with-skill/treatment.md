# Treatment — Pocketfern: each item finds its person

The viewer starts with one shared receipt and finishes knowing that Pocketfern offers split by item, on iOS and Android, free for all users, for receipts up to 50 items. A synthetic, explicitly labelled illustration explains the idea; it does not prove the product's actual interface or interaction.

| Concept | Operation and proof | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Product in use | Show a real receipt assignment and result in the app. | Strongest proof, but no capture or verified workflow supplied. |
| Relational analogy | Receipt lines travel toward their respective people; each line retains its identity. | Could imply unsupported automation unless labelled illustration. |
| Reframe | One bill becomes a set of individual choices, then the feature is named. | Needs a concrete example to communicate item-level splitting. |

Choose the relational analogy, borrowing the real-product sequence if verified capture becomes available. Governing operation: one receipt remains recognisable while its individual lines separate toward their people. The analogy stops at the concept of assignment; it makes no claim about the gesture, automation, payment or calculation. Receipt identity stays stable; grouping changes.

Tests: the minimal operation reads as individual items assigned to different people; synthetic artwork is labelled “Illustration”; a newcomer should be able to say “split a shared receipt by item”; the receipt and feature name distinguish it from a generic equal split; cause, result, brand and facts remain visible muted. These are hypotheses pending first-viewer tests, not verified outcomes.

Structure: shared receipt → the turn at item assignment → peak when distinct items settle under two people → feature and availability. Ending gives name, platforms and price, without an unprovided link or download destination. The receipt line is the thread. Warm paper and dark ink with a single fern-green accent are provisional art direction, not claimed brand colours. No exact logo or interface will be invented.

Sound: optional locally sourced or properly licensed light rhythmic bed; soft selection accents only on meaningful assignments, then a restrained finish. Action leads the demo; silence or sustained music leaves fact cards readable. No voice. Final sound requires listening review.

Cover: first frame, “Pocketfern” and “Split by item” beside a recognisable illustrative receipt. Format each rendition separately: landscape can place text beside the receipt; portrait stacks the title and receipt, with essential content away from Story overlays. Exact text placement and size must be checked at mobile viewing size.

Open evidence: product capture or verified workflow and brand assets would support an authentic demo. Confirm the CTA destination if one is desired. The example illustration should be reviewed against actual behaviour before it is used to represent it.
