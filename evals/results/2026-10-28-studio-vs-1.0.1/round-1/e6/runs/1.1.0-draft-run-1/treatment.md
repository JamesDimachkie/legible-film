# Treatment — provisional

The change: a viewer sees that one shared receipt can be split by item in Pocketfern, and learns the 50-item limit and free availability. Evidence will be the real interface; the film does not establish the exact steps, automatic scanning, payment settlement, speed, or error-free recognition.

Three directions, to be compared as rough moving sketches before selection:

1. **The receipt resolves** (recommended): a shared receipt is introduced, then the actual product interaction reveals individual item assignments and the resulting split. Peak: the verified result. Risk: depends on the real workflow and a readable capture.
2. **One receipt, many parts**: receipt lines separate visually into groups, then a real product view grounds the analogy. Risk: the animation could imply automatic assignment; label illustration and show the actual action.
3. **A better question**: “Who had what?” introduces an item-by-item demonstration. Risk: wording or a staged workflow may imply unsupported pain or functionality.

Provisional governing operation: one receipt becomes an intelligible set of item-level allocations, with the real product action causing the change. Keep the receipt and identities stable; do not animate an unverified result. The analogy ends at the real interface.

Tests, pending real capture and viewer review: operation—one line and one assignment should communicate the action; truth—only verified states demonstrate behaviour; decoding—a new viewer should identify the product and operation muted; specificity—the receipt and item-level split are intrinsic to this feature, although shared-budget competitors may also offer it. Falsifiability: if the interface does not show a verified item-level assignment and resulting split, the proof fails. No sketch or concept has been approved.

Structure: question, demonstration, proof, availability. Peak at the verified result. End with product name and availability, leaving enough time to read. Copy and timing are provisional; see score.csv. Density moves from one receipt to visible individual items, then a simple end card. Design direction, type, colour, motion and music wait for brand materials and visual options.
