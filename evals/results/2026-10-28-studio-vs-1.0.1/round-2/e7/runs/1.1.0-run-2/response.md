# Atlasfold launch film: plan

The proposed film follows a folder of photos through Atlasfold into a searchable map. It is designed to work with the sound off, then reward a sounded viewing with a restrained music and effects track. The proof should be an actual product run, so the audience can see what Atlasfold really does.

Three directions are ready to explore: the folder becoming a map; one photo finding its place in a larger picture; and a specific search question resolving on screen. I recommend the first as the starting hypothesis because it makes the input and output clear. The other two depend on product behavior that has not yet been demonstrated. All three are described in [treatment.md](treatment.md), with a provisional 45-second sequence in [score.csv](score.csv).

The proposed delivery is one 1920×1080, 16:9 MP4 for the README and LinkedIn, with a cover image and captions if there is speech. The next steps are: verify the product and study comparable films; make three rough moving sketches for a story choice; show stills for the look; make a timed rough of the whole film; compare sound options; then finish and check the approved cut. At each stage you will see what is temporary and what decision it affects. The study and all creative approvals remain open; this run produced a plan, not a video.

The $400 cap is recorded in [budget.csv](budget.csv). The provisional allocations are $120 for music, $100 for voice, $80 for optional stock or generation, and $100 in reserve, all including tax. They are ceilings for planning, not purchases or approved commitments. **Spent and committed: $0.** Before any purchase or paid generation, I would present the exact item, full cost and licence for your approval. Voice and stock may be unnecessary; the real product demonstration is the priority.

The claims in [facts.json](facts.json) come from your brief and still need checking against the release and repository. The study of comparable films and current platform requirements is also incomplete, as recorded in [study.md](study.md). The weakest point is that the real command, map and search behavior are not yet available to inspect.

I can prepare and measure the film, but I cannot watch motion at normal speed or hear the mix. A person must watch the finished file muted and listen with sound before release; until then it will be reported as unwatched and unheard. The review will test whether viewers understand the product, recognize its particular action, find the experience worthwhile, see an earned payoff, draw accurate conclusions and remember Atlasfold. We will revise the weakest moment until those questions are met or you stop the process.
